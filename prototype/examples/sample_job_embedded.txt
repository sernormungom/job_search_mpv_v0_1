Senior Embedded Software Engineer - Gothenburg, Hybrid

We are looking for a senior embedded software engineer to join an automotive development team in Gothenburg.
You will develop embedded software in C++ and C, work with AUTOSAR, Vector DaVinci and CI/CD pipelines, and contribute to verification and validation.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and integrate embedded software for safety-critical vehicle systems.
- Work in Agile teams using Jira, Git and Jenkins.
- Participate in requirements analysis, implementation, unit testing, SIL and HIL verification.
- Collaborate with system architects and test engineers.

Requirements:
- Strong C++ experience.
- Experience with embedded systems and real-time software.
- Experience from automotive or other safety-critical domains.
- Knowledge of ISO 26262, AUTOSAR, Vector tools, CI/CD, SIL or HIL is desirable.
- Swedish is a plus, English is required.
